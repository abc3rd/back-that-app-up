Uncompromising Engineering

Built for spontaneous minds & absolute privacy

Designed to run silently in the background of your daily life without consuming battery, storage, or intruding on your private conversations.

RAM-Only Buffer Engine

Audio continuously writes to a volatile rolling memory loop and discards itself every few minutes. Zero data touches your SSD or flash storage until a trigger fires.

Automatic Spike Detection

Set your custom decibel threshold. Sudden laughter, unexpected applause, or a sudden burst of sound automatically saves the context around it.

Hands-Free Voice Arming

Say your custom wake phrase like "Back That Up!" or "Save That Quote!" to retroactively secure what was just said without pulling out your phone.

AI On-Device Transcription

Turn saved audio clips into indexed, searchable text instantly. Search your library by the exact words spoken weeks ago with full keyword matching.

Rich Context Metadata

Every saved moment automatically attaches exact timestamps, trigger method tags, decibel peaks, and optional GPS coordinates for instant context retrieval.

Zero-Knowledge Cloud Backup

Keep files strictly local or opt to auto-sync encrypted copies to your personal Google Drive or preferred cloud storage solution effortlessly.
